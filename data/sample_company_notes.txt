Q3 2026 Internal Notes — Fernbridge Software Inc.

Customer churn rose to 4.2% in Q3, up from 2.9% in Q2. The main driver was the
Starter-tier segment, where support response time averaged 36 hours, well above
the 12-hour SLA target. Exit interviews cited slow support and missing export
features as the top two reasons for cancellation.

The Enterprise tier grew net revenue by 18% quarter over quarter, driven mostly
by three renewals with expanded seat counts. No enterprise account churned in Q3.

Engineering shipped the CSV export feature in late September, after most of the
Q3 churn had already happened, so its impact on retention won't be visible until
the Q4 numbers.

Headcount: Support team grew from 4 to 6 people in Q3 specifically to address
the response-time gap. Full effect expected in Q4.
